Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 September 2025 · Last updated: 1 September 2025

VittaBodh ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the VittaBodh explainable loan assessment platform ("Service"). Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and phone number (optional) provided during registration.
  • Financial Data: Monthly income, expenses, savings balance, investment value, EMI, loan details, credit score, and other fields required for loan assessment.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, browser type, device type, and IP address for service improvement.
  • Assessment History: Records of all loan assessments you have submitted, including results and explanations.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To perform explainable loan risk assessments using our Deep Neural Network and ELRS engine.
  • To generate AI-powered explanations and improvement suggestions.
  • To maintain your assessment history and provide downloadable PDF reports.
  • To authenticate your account and maintain session security via Firebase.
  • To improve our models, detect fraud, and ensure platform reliability.
  • To communicate important updates about the Service.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption (256-bit AES). All connections to our platform use TLS/HTTPS encryption. Financial data submitted for assessments is stored in our secured database and is accessible only to you and authorised platform administrators.

We employ Firebase Authentication for identity management, which provides enterprise-grade security including email verification and secure token handling.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data and assessment history for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at privacy@vittabodh.ai.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal or financial information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google), Google Cloud (Gemini AI) for authentication and AI explanation generation, bound by strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

6. AI and Automated Decision-Making

VittaBodh uses automated machine learning models to generate loan assessment results. These results are for informational purposes only and do not constitute formal lending decisions. All outputs are explainable and auditable. We do not use protected characteristics (race, religion, gender, etc.) as primary scoring inputs.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: Request your assessment data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@vittabodh.ai.

8. Cookies

We use only essential session cookies for authentication and security. No third-party tracking or advertising cookies are used.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes via email. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact:
